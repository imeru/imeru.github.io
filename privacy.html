Nest ObserverPrivacy policy

Privacy policy · Effective October 17, 2026

Your home data stays out of this website.

This policy describes the planned and configured data practices of Nest Observer, a personal-use thermostat observation application. Google account linking is currently being configured.

Information accessed

With the owner's authorization, the local application accesses selected Nest device identifiers and metadata, indoor temperature, humidity when available, heating setpoints, HVAC activity, and Eco status. It stores OAuth credentials needed to maintain the authorized connection. A weather service may receive an approximate location selected by the owner to provide local weather.

Purpose and permissions

Data is used to observe heating behavior, investigate setpoint changes, and develop local thermal models. The Home Assistant integration can expose control capabilities, but the initial observation phase does not send thermostat control commands. Permissions are not represented as technically read-only.

Storage and retention

Device records and credentials are stored in the owner's local Home Assistant configuration directory on a Mac. The recorder is configured to retain history for 180 days. Backups and exported datasets, if created, require separate deletion by the owner. Operation is planned through February 2027, followed by a review of export, deletion, and authorization revocation.

Sharing and service providers

Device records are not sold, used for advertising, or published on this website. Google processes API requests and device events; Home Assistant runs locally. This public information site is hosted separately on GitHub Pages and does not receive the application's device records or OAuth tokens. No thermostat data transfer to a hosted AI model is configured.

Website visitors

These pages contain no analytics scripts, forms, or application-level cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata, such as IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure the site.

Access, revocation, and deletion

The owner can review locally stored records, revoke the application's access through Google Account settings, and remove the Nest integration in Home Assistant. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself erase existing records. To delete stored data, the owner must remove the relevant local records, credentials, exports, and backups.

Questions and changes

This application is not offered to outside users. The sole intended user is also its operator and can manage data directly in the local installation. For authorization-related contact information, refer to the support email displayed in the Google OAuth consent configuration. This policy will be updated before materially different data practices are introduced.
