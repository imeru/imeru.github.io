Privacy policy · Effective October 17, 2026 · Last updated October 17, 2026

Your home data stays out of this website.

This policy describes the planned and configured data practices of Nest Observer, a personal-use thermostat observation application. Google account linking is active, and the application is in its observation phase.

Information accessed

With the owner's authorization, the local application accesses selected Nest device identifiers and metadata, indoor temperature, humidity when available, heating setpoints, HVAC activity, and Eco status. It stores OAuth credentials needed to maintain the authorized connection. Weather services (Open-Meteo and the U.S. National Weather Service) receive only an approximate city-level location to provide local weather.

Household presence

With the consent of the household members, a scanner on the owner's Mac checks every five minutes whether the family's phones are connected to the home Wi-Fi network. The phones' Wi-Fi (MAC) addresses are stored only in a protected file on that Mac. The observation database records only a household label (such as adult1 or child1) and whether that phone was seen. It does not record locations outside the home, and a phone left at home or temporarily disconnected can make the record inaccurate.

Purpose and permissions

Data is used to observe heating behavior, investigate setpoint changes, relate heating to whether household members are home, and develop local thermal models and simulations. The Home Assistant integration can expose control capabilities, but the initial observation phase does not send thermostat control commands. Permissions are not represented as technically read-only.

Storage and retention

Device records and credentials are stored in the owner's local Home Assistant configuration directory on a Mac. The recorder is configured to retain history for 180 days. Observation and presence records are kept on the Mac for the duration of the project. Encrypted Home Assistant backups and daily observation exports and database snapshots are copied to the owner's personal Microsoft OneDrive storage; the exports and snapshots are not separately encrypted. Backups and exported datasets require separate deletion by the owner. Operation is planned through February 2027, followed by a review of export, deletion, and authorization revocation.

Sharing and service providers

Device records are not sold, used for advertising, or published on this website. Google processes API requests and device events; Home Assistant runs locally. Microsoft stores the backup copies described above in the owner's OneDrive account. This public information site is hosted separately on GitHub Pages and does not receive the application's device records or OAuth tokens. To prepare daily quality-check reports for the owner, observation summaries and logs are processed by Anthropic's Claude, a hosted AI service, under the owner's account. OAuth credentials, access tokens, and phone Wi-Fi addresses are excluded from that processing.

Website visitors

These pages contain no analytics scripts, forms, or application-level cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata, such as IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure the site.

Access, revocation, and deletion

The owner can review locally stored records, revoke the application's access through Google Account settings, and remove the Nest integration in Home Assistant. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself erase existing records. To delete stored data, the owner must remove the relevant local records, credentials, exports, and backups.

Questions and changes

This application is not offered to outside users. The sole intended user is also its operator and can manage data directly in the local installation. For authorization-related contact information, refer to the support email displayed in the Google OAuth consent configuration. This policy will be updated before materially different data practices are introduced.
